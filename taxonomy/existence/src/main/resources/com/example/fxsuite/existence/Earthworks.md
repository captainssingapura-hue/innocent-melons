# Earthworks

The second part of the made layer: the ground itself reshaped by people, laid over the networks. An earthwork changes
the ground's height and stands on nothing; a building stands on the ground and changes nothing of it. A kind says what
the work does, never what it is dug with.

## Earthwork

The ground reshaped by people.

## Levelled

Ground made level, to be built on or farmed.

## Pad

A level site made for a building to stand on, its ground cut and filled.

## Terrace

A level step made in a slope, for fields or for building.

## Reclaimed

Land made where water was, filled and levelled.

## Raised

Earth heaped up.

## Embankment

A bank raised to carry a way, or to hold the ground beside one.

## Dike

A bank raised to hold water back.

## Mound

A heap of earth raised for its own sake: a barrow, a lookout.

## CityWall

A rampart round a town, its earth rammed in layers, walked along its top.

## Cut

Earth dug away.

## Cutting

A way dug through rising ground.

## Ditch

A narrow trench, to drain the ground or to mark a bound.

## Moat

A wide ditch round a town or a castle, often holding water, to keep out.

## Faced

A bank given a face of stone, to hold it against the water.

## Revetment

A river's or a canal's bank faced in stone.

## Seawall

A wall of stone or concrete holding the land against the sea.

## Cultivated

Ground worked to grow crops.

## Field

Ground ploughed and sown.

## Paddy

A field kept flooded, to grow rice.
