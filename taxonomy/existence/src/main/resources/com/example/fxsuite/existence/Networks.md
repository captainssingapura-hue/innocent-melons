# Networks

The first part of the made layer: what people and goods go along, and what carries them where the ground cannot. Laid
before the earthworks and the buildings, which are laid out along them. A kind says what a way is for, never how it is
built: a road is a road in earth or in asphalt.

## Network

A part of the ways people have made across the land.

## Way

A way along the ground, gone along on foot, by cart or by car.

## Road

A way between places, across the country.

## Street

A way through a town, lined with what is built along it.

## Lane

A narrow way, between fields or behind houses.

## Path

A way for walking only.

## Towpath

A path along a river's or a canal's bank, from which boats are hauled.

## Waterway

A way for boats, dug by people.

## Canal

A channel dug to carry boats, its water held at a level.

## Carrier

What holds a way where the ground cannot: over water, over another way, through a hill.

## Bridge

Carries a way over water or over another way.

## Viaduct

Carries a way a long way over low ground, on many spans.

## Tunnel

Carries a way through a hill or under water.

## Ford

Carries a way across a shallow water, along its bed.

## Pavement

What a way is surfaced with, laid by people.

## Paving

Flat stones or slabs, laid close.

## Setts

Squared stones laid in rows.

## Asphalt

Stone bound in bitumen, laid hot and rolled.

## Gravel

Small stones spread and rolled.
