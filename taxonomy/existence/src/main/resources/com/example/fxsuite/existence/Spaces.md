# Spaces

The parts of a building one can be in, and move between. A space says what kind of place it is, never what it is used
for: that is its use.

## Space

A part of a building one can be in.

## Enclosed

Walled and roofed.

## Room

A space of its own, with a door.

## Hall

A large room, open across, for many.

## ShopFloor

Open to the street along its front, a counter or goods at it.

## OfficeFloor

A storey's open floor round its core, to be set out with desks.

## Unit

A let part of a storey with its own door: a flat, a hotel room, a suite.

## Open

Under the sky, or open at its sides.

## Courtyard

Open ground with the building round it.

## Terrace

A level floor in the open, at a setback or on a roof.

## Deck

A floor carried in the open: a bridge's, a platform's.

## Grounds

Planted ground belonging to a building.

## CoveredWay

A roofed walk along a front, open to the street: a shophouse's five-foot way.

## Circulation

For getting from one space to another.

## Entrance

Where a building is come into from outside.

## Stair

Steps between storeys, and their landings.

## LiftLobby

Where lifts are waited for and left.

## Corridor

A walk between rooms.

## Gateway

A way through a wall or under a building.

## Ramp

A sloping way between levels.

## Servicing

For what serves the building, not its people.

## Core

A tower's lifts, stairs and risers, gathered.

## Shaft

A void running up through storeys: a lift's, a riser's.

## PlantRoom

A room of machinery.

## Washroom

Toilets and basins.
