# Uses

What a storey or a space is for, whoever built it and whenever. A use says what happens there, never how it looks.

## Use

What a storey or a space is for.

## Dwelling

Lived in.

## Homes

Homes people live in: flats in a tower, the rooms of a house.

## Penthouses

The largest homes, at a tower's top.

## ServicedHomes

Homes let furnished by the week or the month, served as a hotel's rooms are.

## Work

Worked in.

## Offices

Desks and rooms for office work.

## CoWorking

Desks and rooms let by the day or the month, shared.

## Studios

Rooms to broadcast or record from.

## MedicalSuites

Doctors' rooms and clinics.

## Trade

Bought and sold in.

## Shops

Goods sold over a counter or from shelves, open to the street or a mall.

## Market

Selling from stalls, in the open or under awnings.

## Banking

A bank's hall, its counters open to its customers.

## Hospitality

Stayed in or eaten and drunk in, by guests.

## HotelRooms

Rooms let by the night, served from a lobby.

## Restaurant

Meals served at tables: a restaurant, an eating house, a tea house.

## Bar

Drink served: a bar, a wine house's rooms.

## Leisure

Rested, played or looked out in.

## Club

Rooms for residents or members: a lounge, a function room.

## Gym

Exercise and wellness: a gym, a spa.

## Pool

A pool to swim in, and its deck.

## Garden

Planted, to walk and sit in: a sky garden, a terrace of trees.

## Observation

A deck or a gallery to look out from, over the city.

## Gallery

Things shown to visitors.

## Assembly

Gathered in, for a purpose.

## Auditorium

Seats facing a stage or a screen.

## EventSpace

A hall let for events, set out as each needs.

## Courts

Courtrooms and the chambers beside them.

## Worship

Prayer and rites.

## Service

What keeps a building working; no one's place to be.

## Plant

Machinery: chillers, pumps, tanks, lift motors.

## Parking

Cars left, and the ramps between its floors.

## Technical

Equipment floors: switching, cabling, broadcast gear.

## Storage

Goods kept: a warehouse, a store room.

## Movement

Passed through, on the way somewhere.

## Lobby

The way in at the ground, where lifts and stairs are taken.

## SkyLobby

Where riders change lifts high up: a transfer floor.

## Crossing

Carrying a way over water or over another way.

## Passage

A way through a wall or under a building: a gate's.

## Defence

Held against those not let in.

## Watch

Looked out from and held: a gate tower's room, a wall's walk.
