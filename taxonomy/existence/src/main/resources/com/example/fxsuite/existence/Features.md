# Features

The layer of the land's features, laid over the base terrain: its waters and their edges, however they came there.
A canal is a river people dug; how a river was made matters to nothing in the world. None of them is built by a
civilization. A kind says what the feature is, never how big.

## Feature

A feature of the land, laid over its terrain.

## Water

Water lying on the land or running over it.

## Flowing

Water running one way along a course of its own.

## River

A large flowing water, deep and wide enough for boats.

## Stream

A small flowing water, waded across.

## Canal

A river dug by people, straight in its reaches, its water held level, deep and wide enough for boats.

## Still

Water lying where the land holds it, running nowhere.

## Lake

A large still water, wide enough for boats.

## Pond

A small still water.

## Marsh

Ground lying wet under shallow water and reeds.

## Open

Water that is the sea's, reaching beyond the land.

## Sea

The open sea.

## Bay

The sea reaching into the land, held round on most sides.

## Edge

Where the land meets the water.

## Bank

The rising ground along a river's or a lake's side.

## Shore

The land along the sea's edge.

## Beach

A shore of sand or shingle, sloping gently into the water.
