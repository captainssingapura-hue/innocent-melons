# Things

What lies about the world and is handled, a piece at a time. A thing says what it is, never what can be done with it:
that is bound to its kind apart, and a kind with nothing bound to it takes what is bound to the kind over it.

## Thing

A thing that lies about the world and is handled.

## Fruit

What grew to be eaten.

## Melon

A large fruit with a hard rind, too big to be eaten as it is.

## Watermelon

A melon striped dark and light green, red within: the engine's first fruit.

## MelonSlice

A piece cut from a melon, small enough to hold and eat.

## Apple

A small fruit eaten as it is, out of the hand.

## Pineapple

A fruit that stands on end under a crown of leaves.

## Plaything

Made to be played with.

## Ball

A round thing to roll, throw and catch.

## Container

Made to hold other things.

## Crate

A box of boards, carried and stacked.

## Piece

A piece of something built, standing or lying by itself.

## Pillar

An upright that holds something up.

## Beam

A long piece laid across from one support to another.

## Chock

A block set against something that would roll or slide, to keep it where it is.

## Ramp

A sloping slab to go up or come down by.

## Fixture

Fixed to something built, and worked where it is.

## Door

A leaf that shuts an opening, and opens to let one through.

## Carrier

Made to carry things from place to place.

## Wagon

A flat carrier that runs on a track.
