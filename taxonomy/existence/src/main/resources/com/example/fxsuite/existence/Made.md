# Made

The made layer: what people have made on the land, laid over its terrain and its natural features. Its three parts are
laid in turn, each over those before it: the networks, then the earthworks, then the buildings. What each part's
nodes mean is beside its holder: `Networks.md`, `Earthworks.md`, `Buildings.md`.

## Made

Made by people on the land, over its terrain and its natural features: by a civilization, in an era.
