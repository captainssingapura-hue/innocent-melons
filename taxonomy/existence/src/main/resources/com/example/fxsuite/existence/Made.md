# Made

The made layer: what people have made on the land, laid over its terrain and its features. Its departments are laid
in turn, each over those before it, in the order each map says: the highways, the bridges, the tunnels, the earthworks,
the buildings. What each department's nodes mean is beside its holder: `Highways.md`, `Bridges.md`, `Tunnels.md`,
`Earthworks.md`, `Buildings.md`. A network, the ways and waters traffic goes along and how they meet, is no part of
it: it builds nothing.

## Made

Made by people on the land, over its terrain and its features: by a civilization, in an era.
