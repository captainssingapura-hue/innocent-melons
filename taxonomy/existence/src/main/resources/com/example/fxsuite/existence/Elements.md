# Elements

The parts a building is made of. An element says what the part does, never what it is made of or how it is shaped: those
are its civilization's and its era's.

## Element

A part a building is made of.

## Support

Carries what is over it down to the ground.

## Column

An upright carrying a load at a point: a column, a post, a pillar.

## BearingWall

A wall carrying the floors or the roof over it.

## Arch

A curve or a polygon of members carrying a load across an opening by thrust.

## Abutment

What an arch's or a bridge's end stands on and pushes against.

## Platform

A raised base a building stands on.

## Bracket

Stepped arms carrying an eave or a beam out past its support.

## Span

Carries a load across, between supports.

## Beam

A member lying across supports.

## Floor

What a storey is stood on.

## Decking

The boards or slab walked on over a structure: a bridge's.

## Rib

One of the members an arch or a vault is made of.

## Cover

Keeps the weather off what is under it.

## Roof

A building's cover.

## Eave

A roof's edge, out past the wall.

## Canopy

A light cover on its own supports: an awning, an umbrella, a porch.

## Crown

What stands over a tower's roof: its screen, its spire.

## Enclosure

Shuts a space in at its sides.

## Wall

A solid side.

## CurtainWall

A skin of glass hung on a frame, carrying nothing but itself.

## Spandrel

The band between one storey's glass and the next, hiding the floor's edge.

## Louvre

Slats letting air through and keeping rain and sight out.

## Screen

A light, open side: lattice, shutters, a balustrade of panels.

## Opening

A way or a view through an enclosure.

## Door

An opening gone through, and its leaf.

## Window

An opening seen through, and its glass or its shutter.

## Shopfront

A shop's open front, shut at night.

## Archway

An opening through a wall for a way to pass: a gate's.

## Guard

Keeps people from an edge.

## Railing

Posts and rails along an edge.

## Parapet

A low wall along an edge: a roof's, a city wall's battlements.

## Conveyance

Takes people from one level to another.

## Steps

A flight of steps.

## LiftBank

Lifts side by side, serving the same floors.

## Incline

A sloping floor walked or driven up.

## Ornament

Marks or announces; carries nothing.

## Pole

A standing pole carrying a sign, a vane or a figure.

## Sign

A board or a banner naming or announcing.

## Finial

A figure or a knob finishing a roof's ridge or a post's top.

## Fitting

Stands in a space to be used; no part of what holds the building up or shuts it in.

## Counter

A table or a counter to sell or to serve from.

## Bench

A seat for more than one.
