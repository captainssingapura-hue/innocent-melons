# Highways

The made layer's department of ways along the ground: what people and goods go along, and what it is paved with. A
kind says what a way is for, never how it is built: a road is a road in earth or in asphalt. A way may be part of a
network, which builds nothing of its own.

## Highway

A way made along the ground, and what it is paved with.

## Way

A way along the ground, gone along on foot, by cart or by car.

## Road

A way between places, across the country.

## Expressway

A road for fast traffic only, its two ways kept apart, come onto and left only where it is joined.

## Street

A way through a town, lined with what is built along it.

## Lane

A narrow way, between fields or behind houses.

## Path

A way for walking only.

## Towpath

A path along a river's or a canal's bank, from which boats are hauled.

## Ford

A way across a shallow water, along its bed.

## Pavement

What a way is surfaced with, laid by people.

## Paving

Flat stones or slabs, laid close.

## Setts

Squared stones laid in rows.

## Asphalt

Stone bound in bitumen, laid hot and rolled.

## Gravel

Small stones spread and rolled.
