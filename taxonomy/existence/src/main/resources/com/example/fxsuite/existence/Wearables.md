# Wearables

What is put on the body and goes with it as it moves, until it is taken off. A wearable says what it is, never how it
is made nor what it is made of.

## Wearable

What is put on the body and goes with it as it moves.

## Garment

Made of cloth, bending as the body bends under it.

## Briefs

A garment over the hips and the groin, open for the legs.

## Tights

A garment close over the hips, the legs and the feet.

## Armour

Hard, worn to take blows: each piece keeps its shape over the part of the body it guards.

## Helmet

Armour over the whole head, open for the neck, a visor across the eyes.

## Cuirass

Armour over the trunk, a breastplate and a backplate, open for the neck and the arms.

## Pauldron

Armour over a shoulder, from its top down the outside of the upper arm.

## Vambrace

Armour round a forearm, from below the elbow to above the wrist.

## Greave

Armour round a shin, from below the knee to above the ankle.
