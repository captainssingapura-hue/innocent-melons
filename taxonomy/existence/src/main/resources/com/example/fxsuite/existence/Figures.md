# Figures

Bodies that move themselves, stand and walk, each seen as one: an avatar, one of a crowd. A figure says what it
is, never how it is made nor what it is made of.

## Figure

A body that moves itself.

## Robot

A machine built as a person is, and moving as one does.

## FramedRobot

A robot its frame bare: its bones of metal, its joints in housings, its spine, its chest's cage and its head machined.

## ShelledRobot

A robot its frame covered: a shell of plates over it, one to each part, parted at the joints so that it bends.
