# Bridges

The made layer's department of what carries a way over a gap: over water, over another way, over low ground. A kind
says what a bridge carries over, never how it is built: a bridge is a bridge in timber, in stone or in steel.

## Span

A way carried over a gap.

## Bridge

Carries a way over water or over another way.

## Viaduct

Carries a way a long way over low ground, on many spans.

## Flyover

Carries a road over another road where they cross, on a few short spans, so neither waits for the other.
