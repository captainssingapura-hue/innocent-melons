# Engine parts

The kinds of part an engine needs, by what each does in it. Each group is split twice: the GLM's own, generic to every
engine its one modular design casts, in line, in a V or flat; and the V2's, the motorcycle twin's own kinds where its
parts are not the GLM's. The V2 takes the GLM's kinds wherever they serve it.

## EnginePart

A part of an engine, by what it does there.

## Blocks

What the cylinders are bored in and the crank is carried by: it holds them where they work against each other, and
carries the engine on its mounts.

## GlmBlocks

The blocks the GLM's modular design casts, each whole, its cylinders bored in it and its crank's main bearings in its
bulkheads.

## InlineBlock

A block for one bank of cylinders standing over the crank.

## VeeBlock

A block for two banks of cylinders over one crank, leaning apart in a V.

## BoxerBlock

A block for two banks lying flat either side of the crank, opposed: a crankcase round the crank with a bank out of each
side.

## CylinderLiner

What a piston runs in, set in its bore in the block: it takes the piston's wear in the block's place.

## V2Blocks

The motorcycle twin's: a crankcase that carries its gearbox as well as its crank, and its cylinders apart from it.

## TwinCrankcase

What carries the twin's crank, its balancer and its gearbox's shafts in one, and the cylinders on it: the engine's
middle, and part of the motorcycle's frame, carrying its loads.

## CylinderBarrel

A cylinder made apart from the crankcase and fixed on it, so it can be taken off or bored bigger.

## Cranks

What turns with the crank, and what goes up and down on it: the pistons' push turned into the crank's turning.

## GlmCranks

The modular design's: what every one of its engines turns on, the V2's among them, but for its crank.

## Crankshaft

What the rods turn: a pin for each throw between main journals, turning in the block's main bearings.

## ConnectingRod

What joins a piston to its pin on the crank, pushing and pulling between them.

## Piston

What the charge's burning pushes down its cylinder, sealing it as it goes.

## GudgeonPin

What a rod's small end swings on in its piston.

## V2Cranks

The motorcycle twin's own: its crank, and what is turned to quiet its shaking.

## SplitPinCrankshaft

A twin's crank with a pin for each cylinder, the two turned apart, so the twin fires as one with a wider V does.

## PrimaryBalancer

What turns against the twin's shaking at the crank's own speed, in the crankcase.

## SecondaryBalancer

What turns against the twin's lesser shaking, at twice the crank's speed, in its front head between the cams.

## Heads

What closes the cylinders over the pistons, and what works the valves that let the charge in and the burnt gas out.

## GlmHeads

The modular design's: a head over each bank, its cams carried over it.

## CylinderHead

What closes a bank's cylinders, with the chamber over each where the charge burns, and its ports in and out.

## CamTower

What carries a head's camshafts over it in their bearings.

## Camshaft

What opens the valves in turn as it is turned, half as fast as the crank.

## Valve

What lets the charge into a cylinder, or the burnt gas out, as its cam opens it and its spring shuts it.

## ValveSpring

What shuts a valve again after its cam has opened it, and keeps it shut.

## V2Heads

The motorcycle twin's own: a head over each cylinder, and what lets it rev high and keep its timing.

## TwinCamHead

What closes one of the twin's cylinders, its inlet cam and its exhaust cam carried in it, a plug either side of its
chamber.

## CamPhaser

What turns a cam on from its chain while it runs, so the valves open earlier or later as the engine wants.

## HydraulicTappet

What its cam pushes on to open a valve, taking up its own clearance with oil, so nobody need set it.

## SodiumValve

An exhaust valve hollow and part filled with sodium, which carries the heat from its head to its stem to be cooled.

## Covers

What closes the engine over what works inside it, keeping its oil in and the dirt out.

## GlmCovers

The modular design's: over its cams, its timing and the whole of its top.

## HeadCover

What closes a head over its cams.

## TimingCover

What closes the front of the engine over the drive from the crank to the cams.

## TrimCover

What is laid over the top of the engine to hide and quieten it.

## V2Covers

The motorcycle twin's own: over its cams, its clutch and its alternator, each light, cast in magnesium.

## CamCover

What closes one of the twin's heads over its cams.

## ClutchCover

What closes the crankcase over the clutch and the primary drive, on one side.

## AlternatorCover

What closes the crankcase over the alternator, on the other.

## Electrics

What fires the charge, makes the engine's current and turns it over to start.

## GlmElectrics

The modular design's: what fires each cylinder's charge, the V2's among them.

## CoilPack

What makes the spark for a plug, sat on it, a coil to each.

## SparkPlug

What sparks across its gap in the chamber to set the charge burning.

## V2Electrics

The motorcycle twin's own: its current made and its starting on the crank inside the crankcase.

## Alternator

What makes the current on the end of the crank as it turns.

## StarterMotor

What turns the engine over to start it.

## Drives

What carries the crank's turning to the cams and to what the engine drives.

## GlmDrives

The modular design's: from the crank to the cams, the V2's guides among them.

## TimingChain

What turns a bank's cams from the crank, at half its speed.

## ChainGuide

What a timing chain runs along on its tight side, keeping it on its line.

## ChainTensioner

What presses on a timing chain's slack side, so it never runs slack however it stretches.

## V2Drives

The motorcycle twin's own: up each cylinder to its head's cams, and on from the crank to its gearbox in the crankcase.

## CamChain

What turns one head's cams from the crank, up its own cylinder's side.

## Fluids

What keeps the oil round the engine and the water through it.

## GlmFluids

The modular design's: its oil gathered, fed and its water moved, the V2's among them.

## OilSump

What gathers the oil under the crank, to be fed round again.

## OilPump

What feeds the oil to the bearings, or draws it back from where it falls.

## WaterPump

What moves the water through the block, the heads and the radiator.

## V2Fluids

The motorcycle twin's own: its oil cooled.

## OilCooler

What cools the oil with the engine's water before it is fed round again.
