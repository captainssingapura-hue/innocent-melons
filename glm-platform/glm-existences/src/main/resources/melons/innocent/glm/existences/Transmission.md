# Transmission

The motorcycle twin's transmission, in its crankcase with it: from the crank by its primary drive to its clutch,
through its clutch to its gearbox, and out of the gearbox to the pulley its belt runs from.

## PrimaryDrive

What carries the crank's turning to the clutch, gear to gear, slowed by the gears' teeth.

## ScissorGear

The gear on the crank that drives the clutch's, made in two halves sprung a little apart, so its teeth fill the
clutch gear's and they never rattle.

## ClutchGear

The gear the crank's drives, round the clutch's basket, turning it.

## WetClutch

What lets the engine turn without driving the gearbox, and takes up the drive gently, its plates running in the
engine's oil.

## ClutchBasket

What the primary drive turns, holding the friction plates by their outer edges, so they turn with it.

## ClutchHub

What turns the gearbox's mainshaft, holding the steel plates by their inner edges, so they turn with it.

## FrictionPlate

A plate faced to grip, turned by the basket, squeezed against the steel plates to drive them.

## SteelPlate

A plain plate turned with the hub, gripped between the friction plates.

## PressurePlate

What squeezes the plates together, pressed by the clutch's springs, and drawn off them when the lever is pulled.

## ClutchSpring

What presses the pressure plate on the plates, so the clutch drives unless it is drawn off.

## SlipperRamp

What lets the plates slip when the back wheel would drive the engine harder than it can be made to turn, and squeezes
them harder when the engine drives: ramps between the hub and the pressure plate.

## Gearbox

What sets how fast the back wheel turns for the engine's speed, one of its six pairs of gears chosen in turn.

## Mainshaft

The shaft the clutch turns, carrying one gear of each pair.

## Countershaft

The shaft the chosen pair turns, carrying the other gear of each pair and turning the output pulley.

## FixedGear

A gear made with its shaft or fixed to it, always turning with it.

## FreeGear

A gear turning freely on its shaft until a sliding gear beside it locks it to the shaft, choosing its pair.

## SlidingGear

A gear turning with its shaft and sliding along it, its dogs locking a free gear beside it to the shaft; it is a
pair's gear too.

## ShiftDrum

What chooses the gear: a drum turned a step at a time by the shift shaft, its grooves moving the forks.

## ShiftFork

What slides a sliding gear along its shaft, its pin in the drum's groove.

## ShiftShaft

What the shift pedal turns, stepping the drum on a gear up or down.

## OutputPulley

What the countershaft turns outside the crankcase, the final drive's belt running from it to the back wheel.
