# Motorcycle parts

The kinds of part a motorcycle is made of round its engine, by what each does in it. Each group's kinds are any
motorcycle's; where the V2's bike, the twin's, is made otherwise, a second group beside them is its own.

## MotorcyclePart

A part of a motorcycle, by what it does there; its engine and gearbox are the engine parts'.

## Frames

What holds the bike together and carries its rider, its engine and its wheels.

## MotoFrames

Any motorcycle's: where its front end steers, its back wheel swings and its rider sits.

## SteeringHead

What the front end turns in, its bearings carrying the fork's yokes, raked back as the bike's figures say.

## Swingarm

What carries the back wheel's axle and swings up and down about its pivot, its shocks holding it.

## Subframe

What carries the seat, the rider and the back of the bodywork behind the main frame.

## EngineMount

What fixes the engine to the frame, or the frame to the engine where the engine carries the load.

## V2Frames

The V2's bike's own: no frame round its engine, its frame bolted to the engine, which carries the load between.

## TrellisFrame

A frame of short straight tubes welded into triangles, from the steering head back to the engine's heads.

## MidStructure

What the swingarm pivots in and the subframe is bolted to, bolted to the back of the engine.

## Suspension

What lets the wheels follow the road while the bike rides level over it.

## MotoSuspension

Any motorcycle's: a telescopic fork in front and shocks behind.

## ForkLeg

One side of the fork, its tube sliding in its slider over its spring and its damping, the front axle at its foot.

## TopYoke

What clamps the fork's legs together over the steering head and carries the bars.

## BottomYoke

What clamps the fork's legs together under the steering head, turning on its stem in the head's bearings.

## RearShock

What holds the swingarm up on its spring and damps its swinging: one each side, or one alone.

## Controls

What the rider steers and rides the bike with, and is told by.

## MotoControls

Any motorcycle's.

## Handlebar

What the rider steers with, clamped to the top yoke.

## HandGrip

Where the rider holds the bars, the right one turning to open the throttle.

## HandLever

What the rider squeezes at the bars: the front brake's on the right, the clutch's on the left.

## FootPeg

Where the rider's feet rest.

## FootPedal

What the rider presses with a foot: the back brake's on the right, the gear shift's on the left.

## Mirror

What shows the rider the road behind.

## Gauge

What tells the rider how fast, in which gear, and how the engine is.

## Wheels

What the bike rolls on.

## MotoWheels

Any motorcycle's.

## CastWheel

A wheel cast in one piece, its rim on spokes from its hub, its hub turning on its axle.

## Tyre

What grips the road round a wheel's rim, round in section so the bike can lean.

## Axle

What a wheel turns on, through its hub and the fork's feet or the swingarm's ends.

## Brakes

What slows the wheels.

## MotoBrakes

Any motorcycle's: discs gripped by calipers, pressed by their master cylinders.

## BrakeDisc

What turns with its wheel to be gripped and slowed.

## BrakeCaliper

What grips its disc between its pads, pressed by the fluid.

## MasterCylinder

What the lever or the pedal presses, pressing the fluid to its caliper.

## FinalDrives

What carries the gearbox's output to the back wheel.

## MotoFinalDrives

Any motorcycle's that drives its back wheel by a belt.

## DriveBelt

A toothed belt from the gearbox's output pulley round the back wheel's.

## RearPulley

What the belt turns, fixed to the back wheel.

## BeltGuard

What covers the belt where a foot or a stone could reach it.

## Bodywork

What the rider sits on, and what covers the rest and keeps the road off it.

## MotoBodywork

Any motorcycle's.

## Seat

Where the rider sits.

## FrontFender

What keeps the front wheel's spray off the bike and the rider.

## RearFender

What keeps the back wheel's spray off the rider and those behind, carrying the number plate and the tail lamp.

## SideCover

What closes the frame's side under the seat.

## V2Bodywork

The V2's bike's own: its fuel where a tank would not be, and a cover where it would.

## AirboxCover

What sits where a tank would, astride the frame, covering the airbox under it.

## UnderseatTank

The fuel tank under the seat, low, so the bike's weight is lower.

## Intakes

What the engine breathes in through.

## MotoIntakes

Any motorcycle's.

## Airbox

What holds the air the engine breathes, quiet and still, feeding its throttle bodies.

## AirFilter

What cleans the air the engine breathes, in its airbox.

## Exhausts

What the burnt gas leaves the engine by.

## MotoExhausts

Any motorcycle's.

## HeaderPipe

What carries one cylinder's burnt gas from its head.

## Collector

Where the header pipes meet, into one.

## Muffler

What quietens the burnt gas before it leaves the bike.

## HeatShield

What keeps the pipes' heat off the rider's legs.

## Cooling

What carries the engine's heat away in its water.

## MotoCooling

Any liquid cooled motorcycle's.

## Radiator

What the engine's water is cooled in by the air through it.

## RadiatorFan

What draws air through the radiator when the bike is slow or still.

## CoolantTank

What holds the water the engine's takes in and lets out as it warms and cools.

## Lamps

What lights the way and shows the bike to others.

## MotoLamps

Any motorcycle's.

## Headlamp

What lights the road ahead.

## TailLamp

What shows the bike from behind, brighter as it brakes.

## Indicator

What flashes to show which way the bike will turn.
