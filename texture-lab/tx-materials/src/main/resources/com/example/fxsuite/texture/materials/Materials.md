# Materials

What made things are made of. Whatever is here is already shaded: a material is added only with how it is drawn, so a
thing may be made of any of these and be drawn. A leaf is a material as it is seen: two kinds of brick whose faces are
nothing alike are two leaves, though both are fired clay. Only the materials something has so far been made of and
drawn in are here.

## Matter

What a made thing is made of.

## Metal

Worked from ore: strong for its thickness, and it takes a polish.

## Gold

A soft, heavy metal that does not tarnish.

## Bronze

Copper alloyed with tin: harder than either.

## Titanium

A light, strong metal that does not rust.

## Gunmetal

A bronze with zinc in it, cast for what must bear wear.

## Steel

Iron with a little carbon in it: strong and cheap, drawn into tube and pressed into sheet.

## Masonry

Stone, or what is made to stand in for stone, laid piece on piece to stand.

## CommonRedBrick

Clay shaped in a mould and fired hard and red, laid in thick joints of pale mortar.

## ChineseGreyBrick

Clay fired and then starved of air as it cools, so it comes out grey: longer and flatter than the red, and laid in
fine joints of white lime.

## Adobe

Earth and straw shaped in a mould and dried in the sun, not fired.

## Rubble

Stones as they are found or broken, fitted together unshaped.

## DressedStone

Stone cut square, to lie in courses.

## Synthetic

Made by a process, not found and not grown.

## Enamel

A hard coat baked onto what it covers.

## Rubber

A tough, springy stuff that gives and comes back.

## GlossPaint

Colour sprayed onto sheet and sealed under a clear, hard lacquer: whatever colour the thing was to be.

## MetallicPaint

Colour with fine flakes of metal lying in it, sprayed onto sheet and sealed under a clear, hard lacquer: it shines
wide and soft where gloss paint only glints, and glitters seen close.

## Glass

Sand melted and cooled clear, rolled into sheets.

## Luminous

What gives light of its own.

## LitGlass

Glass with a light behind it.

## Flame

Gas, burning.

## Grown

What grew, and was not made.

## Rind

The tough outer skin of a fruit.

## Liquid

What flows, and lies level where it is held.

## Water

What rain is, lying still where the ground or a rim holds it.
