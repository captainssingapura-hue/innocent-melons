# Conditions

The state a surface is in at a spot. It changes with the weather and with what happens there, where the surface's
form and what it is made of do not; and it is a fact of each spot, so a puddle lies on a dry road.

## Condition

The state a surface is in at a spot.

## Bare

Nothing on it but itself.

## Dry

Bare and dry: the surface as its material alone makes it.

## Slick

Something on it that lets go of what presses on it.

## Wet

Wetted: a film of water, which most things grip less well through.

## Icy

Under ice or frost, which very little grips.

## Covered

Something lying over it, between it and what is on it.

## Loose

Strewn with what shifts underfoot: grit, sand, leaves, snow not yet packed.

## Flooded

Under standing water to some depth, which is waded or driven through.
