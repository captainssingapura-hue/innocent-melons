# Surfaces

The forms a surface that is moved over may have: its geometry, apart from what it is made of and what state it is in.
A road, a floor, a stair and a roof are things that provide surfaces of these forms, not forms themselves.

## Surface

The form of a surface that is moved over.

## Firm

It holds what is on it: a thing stands on it and does not sink.

## Level

Flat, and square to down: a floor, a deck, a flat roof, a platform.

## Slope

Flat, and tilted: a ramp, a pitched roof, a road on a grade.

## Stepped

Rising in steps, each of one rise and run: a stair, terraces, a kerb. A foot takes it and a wheel does not.

## Land

Rolling, with a height at each spot: open ground.

## Yielding

It gives way to what is on it, which sinks until it floats or finds the bottom.

## Liquid

Level and yielding: still or moving water.

## Climbed

Gone up and not along.

## Upright

To be climbed and not walked: a ladder, a wall with holds.
